## Text cleaning (`clean_v1`)

- Whitespace is collapsed; casing and punctuation are kept.
- A sentence is removed when more than half of its tokens contain the de-identification mark `XXXX`;
  otherwise the marked tokens are deleted. 1,345 eligible studies (42%)
  contained the mark and were changed by this rule.
- **Side effect (reviewed at G1, rule kept):** deleting a marked word often breaks grammar, because
  the de-identification also hid ordinary anatomy words, and occasionally strengthens a statement
  ("No XXXX infiltrates" becomes "No infiltrates"). Dropping every marked sentence instead would remove
  about 14% of all report words, including finding sentences, so `clean_v1` is kept. Silver probes
  never use a sentence this rule changed.
- Cleaned FINDINGS length: median 29 words, 5th to 95th percentile
  14 to 56, maximum 166.
- **Repeated reports:** 900 eligible studies (28%) have cleaned FINDINGS text
  identical to at least one other study (2,439 distinct texts in total). The commonest single
  text occurs 49 times. These are templated reports for different studies, so
  the same reference text can appear in training and in evaluation splits.
- **`needs_prior` (`prior_v2`):** 832 eligible studies (26%)
  contain one of the fixed words (compared, prior, previous, unchanged, stable, interval, again, previously, redemonstrat*, persist*, remain, remains, remained, resolved, no change, since, now, comparison; * matches any ending). They stay in
  every split; results are also reported without them, and they never become probes. Words that
  describe appearance rather than an earlier exam (increased, decreased, change, old, chronic, healed)
  are deliberately not in the list; 134 further studies contain only such words.
- **Templated reports in evaluation:** text metrics are also reported on the evaluation studies whose
  reference text never occurs in training ("novel reference" subset).

## Limitations

- **No patient identifier.** IU X-ray identifies studies, not patients, so a study is treated as the
  patient unit. Two studies of one patient could fall in different splits; this cannot be checked.
- **Adult-only eligibility cannot be verified**: there is no age field.
- **One source.** All three sites come from the same institution's data. Site differences are created
  by the prevalence table above, not by real differences in scanners, populations or reporting style.
- **Small evaluation splits.** Each site has roughly 100 development and 100 test studies, so per-site
  rates carry wide uncertainty.
- **Group labels are coarse.** One group per study, first match wins; a study with cardiomegaly and
  an opacity counts as `cardio_pleural`.
- **Possible base-model exposure.** IU X-ray is public; a web-trained model may have seen it.
